How it works
Tap Fill: choose a palette color and tap a closed numbered area.

Traditional Acrylic: start inside an area. The paint can cover its visible line and number, but is clipped by the hidden region so it cannot spill into the neighboring part.

Eraser: erase color without touching the black line art.
Choose a color and paint inside the fish. Paint over the lines and numbers; the hidden region keeps the stroke inside the part where you begin.
