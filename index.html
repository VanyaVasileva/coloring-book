How it works
Tap Fill: choose a palette color and tap a closed numbered area.

Acrylic Pen: start drawing inside an area. Your stroke is clipped to that same closed region, so it cannot cross the line.

Eraser: erase color without touching the black line art.
Choose a color and paint inside the fish. The stroke stays clipped to the region where you begin.
